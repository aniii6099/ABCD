FAQ

Questions brands usually ask.

What's the minimum number of cabs?
Campaigns start at 25 cabs. Most local businesses begin with 25 to 50, and city-wide launches use the full network of 200+.
Can I choose which areas my ad covers?
Yes. We group drivers by the areas they usually drive in, so we can focus your campaign on the neighbourhoods that matter to you, from HITEC City to the Old City.
How do I know my ad is actually running?
You get photo proof of every installation, regular route reports, and QR scan data if your creative includes a code.
Do you design the ads?
Yes. Our team adapts your brand creative for wraps, roof boards and seat-backs, and you approve everything before it's printed.
Is the branding safe for the cars?
We use removable, paint-safe vinyl and fittings. Nothing is permanent and drivers' cars are left as they were after the campaign.
How quickly can a campaign start?
Usually within 48 hours of design approval, depending on the number of cabs and the formats you choose.
