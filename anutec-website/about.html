Our story

It started with one cab
and a simple question.

What if the cars that move through Hyderabad all day could carry the city's stories too? Here's how a small idea became a network of more than 200 driver partners.

01
Chapter one · The idea

Stuck at a signal in Madhapur

Every day, thousands of people sit in traffic looking at the car in front of them. Billboards come and go, but that car stays in view for minutes. We saw that it was the most-looked-at surface in the city, and nobody was using it well.

02
Chapter two · The first partners

A handful of drivers who said yes

We didn't start with a fleet. We started with conversations at cab stands, over chai, with drivers who wanted to earn a little more from the hours they already put in. Our first campaign ran on a few cabs, and businesses saw people talking about it.

03
Chapter three · Word spreads

Drivers brought drivers

Our best recruiters were our partners. Regular payouts and respectful treatment travel fast across cab stands. The network spread from the IT corridor to Secunderabad, the Old City and out to the airport.

04
Chapter four · Today

0 driver partners and counting

Today Anutec runs campaigns for brands, hospitals, food chains, real-estate developers and organisations across 30+ areas of Hyderabad. Each campaign is planned around routes, so your ad reaches the people who matter to you.
